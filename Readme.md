
# SurgeSource – B2B Supplier Discovery & Procurement Platform

SurgeSource is a web-based B2B procurement platform designed to simplify product discovery and supplier sourcing for businesses. It enables buyers to explore products, connect with suppliers, request quotations, and compare offers to make informed purchasing decisions.
Buyers create structured Requests for Quotation (RFQs) with required quantities, delivery locations, and deadlines.
uppliers discover commercial opportunities, search and filter open requests, and submit structured quotation bids with delivery lead times and pricing.
The platform aims to improve procurement efficiency, particularly during periods of increased demand when businesses need to identify alternative suppliers and source products quickly.

##  Key Features

- **Product Discovery:** Browse and search products across different categories.
- **Intelligent Alternative Recommendations:** Suggest similar products using product attributes, category relevance and availability when the requested item is out of stock.
- **Supplier Management:** Explore supplier profiles and available product listings.
- **Request for Quotation (RFQ):** Enable buyers to submit quotation requests to suppliers.
- **Quotation Comparison:** Compare supplier offers based on pricing, quantity, and delivery timelines.
- **Procurement Dashboard:** Manage product requests and track quotation statuses.

## Project Objective

The goal of SurgeSource is to make B2B sourcing more accessible and efficient by bringing product discovery, supplier information, and quotation management into a unified platform.
RFQ Creation: Form with validation (product name, description, positive quantity, delivery location, future deadline).
RFQ Management: View complete RFQ specifications, received quotation bids, edit RFQ details, toggle status (OPEN / CLOSED), and delete RFQs.
rowse & Discovery: Browse open RFQs with keyword search across product name, description, and location.
Filters: Filter by delivery location, status (OPEN, CLOSED, ALL), and deadline (Active, Expired, All).
RFQ Details & Quote Submission: View full buyer specifications and submit competitive bids.
